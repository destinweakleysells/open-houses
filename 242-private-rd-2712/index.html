Lot and lake

0.88 acres with pond frontage

The yellow outline in the listing photo marks the approximate lot. The pond sits behind the home and Lake Bob Sandlin is beyond it. Confirm boundaries with a survey.

The Peninsulas at Lake Bob Sandlin

Gated community on the Mount Pleasant side of the lake

Residents have access to about 9,000 acres of boating, fishing and swimming water, and 3.3 miles of shoreline hiking trails. Dallas is about 2 hours away and Texarkana is about 1 hour.
